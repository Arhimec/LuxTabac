# Story: 002-luxembourg-cigarette-catalog

## Metadata
- **Unit**: 001-tip-and-catalog-refinements
- **Intent**: 003-admin-control-and-quota-governance
- **Priority**: Must Have
- **Status**: Planned

## Description
Expand the official cigarette catalog to include all major manufactured cigarette brands available in the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg while rigorously removing all chopped tobacco, rolling tobacco pouches, and volume buckets.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. The catalog contains only manufactured filter cigarettes (cartons of 10x20 and packs of 20).
2. Brands represented with official Luxembourg pricing:
   - Marlboro (Red, Gold, Silver Blue, White Mint)
   - Camel (Yellow, Blue, Essential, Compact)
   - Lucky Strike (Original Red, Blue, Amber)
   - Winston (Classic Red, Blue, Silver, White)
   - Gauloises (Blondes Bleues, Blondes Rouges, Brunes)
   - Chesterfield (Red, Blue, Orange)
   - Philip Morris (Red, Blue)
   - Dunhill (International, Red, Blue)
   - Kent (Core Blue, Silver, White)
   - Vogue (Bleue, Lilas)
   - Davidoff (Classic, Gold, Magnum)
   - Benson & Hedges (Gold, Silver)
   - Pall Mall (Red, Blue)
   - John Player Special (JPS) (Red, Black, Blue)
   - Fortuna (Rouge, Bleue)
   - News (Red, Blue)
   - Ducados (Rubio, Negro)
   - L&M (Red Label, Blue Label)
   - Peter Stuyvesant (Red, Blue)
3. No chopped, rolling, or volume tobacco items appear in the dropdown or price explorer.
4. Each entry displays official per-pack price, per-carton (10x) price, and cigarette count (20 per pack, 200 per carton).
