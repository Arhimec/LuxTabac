# Unit Brief: Promotional Cross-Category Showcase

## Purpose

Feature a high-impact, curated promotional section showcasing Luxembourg's best-selling non-tobacco tax-advantaged goods—specifically premium European coffee pods (Senseo, Nespresso compatible, Dolce Gusto, Lavazza, etc.)—complete with brand, quantity/pack size, and pricing.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Build a responsive promotional card grid section in `index.html`.
- Display top coffee pod selections:
  - Senseo Classic / Corsé (Pads / Pods — e.g., 36 / 54 pads per megabox).
  - Lavazza Espresso Crema e Gusto / Qualità Oro (Nespresso compatible capsules).
  - Nescafé Dolce Gusto Lungo / Espresso Intenso (16 / 30 capsules).
  - Dallmayr Prodomo / Jacobs Krönung Coffee Pods.
- For each item, display: Brand, Format / Pack quantity, Luxembourg competitive price, and tax advantage highlight.
- Clear customer inquiry callout (e.g., "Add to your order note or message our courier directly via Revolut reference").

### Out of Scope
- Adding non-tobacco products to the strict tobacco banderole legal calculation engine.
- External e-commerce stock management integration.
